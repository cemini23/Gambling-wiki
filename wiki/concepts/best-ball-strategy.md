## Raw Concept

Best ball / draft-and-hold fantasy — roster construction across large entry portfolios (Underdog, DraftKings Best Ball, etc.).

## Narrative

### Mechanics

- Draft full roster once; **optimal lineup auto-selected** each week
- No waivers, no trades — pure **draft skill + portfolio variance**
- Large-field tournaments (Best Ball Mania) = extreme variance

### Strategy themes

1. **Portfolio drafting** — many entries, correlated but diversified builds
2. **ADP value** — draft players below consensus ADP when projection disagrees
3. **Stack rules** — QB/WR pairs, bring-back correlates
4. **Bye week construction** — avoid roster cliffs in playoff weeks (weeks 15–17 formats vary)
5. **Bankroll** — treat entry fees like GPP portfolio; see `@concepts/bankroll-management.md`

### Overlap with sports betting

Same NFL knowledge base; best ball is **not** against the spread — player prop and game environment analysis still informs stacks.
