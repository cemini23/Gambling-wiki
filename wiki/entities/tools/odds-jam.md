## Raw Concept

Odds Jam product **Prediction Insiders** — Polymarket wallet copy alerts. Also covers traditional +EV sportsbook tools in broader Odds Jam suite.

## Narrative

**Prediction Insiders** surfaces trades from high-ROI Polymarket wallets with suggested sizing. `@osint-wiki` documents a 30-day $1k experiment: modest profit, underperformed marketing stats, high trade count, variance.

**Retail lessons:** treat as **signal not system**; apply 3-month track record rule; combine with `@concepts/bankroll-management.md`. Traditional Odds Jam (+EV scanner, promos) is separate product line — Phase-0 on ingest.
